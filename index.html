The competitive idea

Don't just sell honey.
Make trust visible.

Execution

A controlled 18-month launch.

M1–M3 · VALIDATE

Compliance gate

Confirm GACC route, official veterinary requirements, importer terms, laboratory testing scope and sample legality.

M4–M6 · PREPARE

Product readiness

Chinese labels, initial test certificates, traceability pages, pilot distributor agreements and packaging.

M7–M12 · LAUNCH

Pilot import

Send conditional pilot shipments only after approvals; measure sell-through, complaints and repeat orders.

M13–M18 · SCALE

Target 20,000 jars

Expand purchase orders when pilot unit economics and compliance are validated; stage inventory against demand.

Go / no-go: if legal entry or importer economics cannot be confirmed by Month 3, pause shipment commitments and re-evaluate the target market rather than treating approval as guaranteed.
